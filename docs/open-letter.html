An open letter to the Prime Minister

Please Put Stability First

Prime Minister,

I voted for the Labour Party partially because I believed the country needed some stability.

Regardless of what issues you may have in your Premiership, none are so pressing as to make your resignation a necessity.

Internal party politics, pressure, and ambition do not necessitate the long-term problems we would inflict on the country if we further endorsed the "revolving door" model of leadership in Number 10.

What is necessary is that you weather the storm in the name of the country that voted for you, and fight the forces that seek to put stability after short, self-serving bursts of diminishing glory.

The possibility of stability got the party my vote. The reality of stability means the party will keep my vote.

The same is true for many others who will never be as loud in your ear as your backbenchers and ministers. I'm sure you don't need reminding as to why keeping the votes of those like me in one place matters now more than ever.

You can do it.

Yours,

Benjamin Hammond
(an ordinary voter)
